Privacy policy

This policy covers the SAHRA Player app. It is the same text the app shows in Settings → Privacy.

SAHRA Player is a media player. It does not provide channels or content.

  • Your playlists, My List, watch history and settings are stored only on this device.
  • Server addresses, usernames and passwords are kept in the device's encrypted secure storage (the Android Keystore on Android devices) and are never written to logs.
  • The app connects only to the servers in your playlists and guide addresses. It sends no analytics, no advertising identifiers and no data to SAHRA.
  • The parental PIN is stored only as a salted hash.
  • Uninstalling the app removes everything it stored.

SAHRA accounts

If you use a SAHRA account (optional):

  • The SAHRA server stores your e-mail address, a hash of your password (or your Google account ID), the devices you sign in on (name, type, last use) and your Premium purchase records from Google Play.
  • TVs send a hashed device ID to check the free trial, so the trial works once per TV.
  • Your playlists, provider logins, channels and watch history are never sent to SAHRA.
  • You can delete your account at any time in Settings → Account → Delete account.

Purchases

SAHRA Premium is bought through Google Play. Google processes the payment; SAHRA receives only the purchase record needed to unlock Premium on your account.
