Delete your SAHRA account

  • In the app: Settings → Account → Delete account. This works on any phone or TV where you are signed in.
  • Without the app: e-mail ahmedemad2210@gmail.com from the address you signed up with, with the subject “Delete my SAHRA account”.

What is deleted

Your account, devices and Premium link are deleted and every device is signed out. This can't be undone, and Premium bought with this account can't be moved to another one. A Google Play subscription keeps billing until you cancel it in Google Play.

Your playlists, provider logins and watch history are never on SAHRA’s server. They stay on your devices until you delete the playlist in the app or uninstall it.
